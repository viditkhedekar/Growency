Cold-email campaign targeting US dental clinics. We will lead with capturing patient enquiries when the front desk is busy or the clinic is closed, supported by the benefit of less administrative work. 


Kairos is an AI front desk built for dental practices. It helps clinics handle patient calls and scheduling, with recall and reporting available as relevant parts of the wider product. For this campaign, let’s try to get the first conversation to focus on answering enquiries and helping patients book when the team is unavailable.
The commercial case is strongest where the clinic already receives enquiries worth capturing. Position Kairos around better access to appointments and practical support for the existing front desk.

Kairos handle implementation.
Kairos will provide the approved subscription price, billing basis, usage limits, setup fees and contract length. The SDR can share those terms when relevant. Trial availability, discounts and multi-location arrangements remain subject to Kairos confirmation.
Do not introduce a free trial, revenue guarantee or fixed implementation deadline. The initial offer is a demonstration, with any evaluation scoped afterward.
Target established, owner-led general dental clinics with 1 to 5 locations, accepting new patients and generating existing enquiries. Practices offering restorative, cosmetic or implant services fit within the same audience. Prioritise reachable owners or managers and a workflow Kairos can support.
Large DSOs, non-dental organisations and practices with no relevant call or scheduling need are lower priority.

- An active new-patient offer or verified advertising activity - someone will need to handle enquiries.
- A current receptionist, scheduling or patient-coordinator vacancy: call coverage can support the practice during hiring and afterward.
- A new dentist, expanded opening hours or another location - any kind of  growth can make appointment access and consistent call handling important.
- Obviously a stated recall backlog is the perfect signal.
These are reasons to start a conversation.
Start with the dentist-owner or practice owner for commercial decisions, and the practice or office manager for daily workflow fit. At a small group, contact the person responsible for operations across the locations. An associate dentist is useful when they can introduce the responsible owner or manager.
Source accounts through clinic websites, map listings, current recruitment pages and practice announcements. Use the clinic's own website to confirm services, locations and patient offers. Record the source and date for the targeting observation. Confirm ownership where a practice may be part of a larger group.
Main angle will be to help the clinic capture patient enquiries during busy periods and after hours. Connect the current call-handling process to suitable appointments and a smoother patient experience.
Supporting angle is to reduce routine phone and scheduling work for the front desk. A hiring signal makes this more timely. 
Use one specific observation about the clinic. For a live patient offer, ask how enquiries are handled after closing. For a reception vacancy, ask how calls are covered during hiring. Mention YC backing or a relevant customer reference briefly to support credibility.
Keep lost appointments and financial impact as hypotheses until the practice confirms them. Lead with its workflow; expand into insurance, recall or other functions when the buyer raises a relevant need.
Offer a short demonstration tailored to the clinic's call and booking workflow. Show how a patient enquiry becomes an appointment, how the practice's scheduling rules are applied, and when the team takes over. Use synthetic patient examples.
Suggested CTA: "Would a short walkthrough of how this could work for your practice be useful?" If the clinic wants to evaluate further, Kairos agrees the location, software, coverage hours, appointment types and commercial terms.

The strongest commercial fit combines existing patient enquiries with capacity to serve them. Consider whether the value of additional attended visits and reduced administrative effort can reasonably justify the approved subscription. A consultation enquiry should not be valued as a completed treatment sale.
A clinic limited by clinical staffing may benefit from workload relief, but extra bookings do not automatically create extra completed visits. Practices with little incoming demand are less suitable for a call-coverage pitch.
A relevant meeting includes an owner or operational evaluator, an established dental practice and a credible reason to assess call handling or scheduling support. Record the system, current process and main concern when available. Unknown budget or call volume need not prevent a useful introduction.
Our team will own account research, cold-email outreach, reply handling, meeting qualification and campaign reporting. Kairos will own demonstrations, technical evaluation, security answers, pricing, pilots, onboarding and closing.
