Business fit is separate from qualification. All 198 retained rows require review; none has every hard acceptance condition verified. Buyer roles are inferred from published product positioning where not explicit. No willingness to buy is implied.

## Sources & Coverage
The current Drive CSV defines the supplied set. Each company has a directly inspected YC profile and an attempted company website. HTTP 200 was returned by 226 websites, but some sites were sparse. A bounded search targeted one matched founder per company; 112 companies yielded dated, identity-matched posts, including 6 within 29 September-29 October 2026. Older posts do not establish current activity. Coverage is confined to this list, not the wider market.

Guest-page followers and engagement are public snapshots observed on 29 October 2026, not a complete live account audit. Company results and customer references are self-reported. Emails are preserved from the input and were not deliverability-tested.
