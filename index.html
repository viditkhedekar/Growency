Questions before the pilot.

The ones we get asked on almost every first call.

What does Growency actually do?

We run outbound for B2B campaigns. We can propose a brief (or use your pre-prepared brief), conduct our thorough research and complete the brief through emails, LinkedIn DMs and introductions and you end up with calls in your calendar.

How is this different from an AI SDR tool?

Tools can only gather research. One of our trained SDRs decides what to write and how to carry the conversation forward.

What happens in the two-week pilot?

We understand what you're selling and who you're selling it to very deeply. Then we create large marketing campaigns, primarily cold emails and cold DMs, send them to all of those people and get you booked calls.

We don't charge anything for the two weeks of the free trial when we get you booked calls.

What does it cost?

We charge for booked calls and positive replies. We do not take commissions or a share of your sales revenue. Rates, scope and qualification criteria are agreed in writing after the pilot. There is no commitment until that point. Explore pricing.

What do you report on?

Formally, every week but we respond promptly regarding any concerns or suggestions you may have.

What about our domain and sending reputation?

We have extensive safeguards in place to prevent spam filtering and harming your domain score. We usually use alternative domains for sending e.g. "usecompany.com" or "trycompany.com".
