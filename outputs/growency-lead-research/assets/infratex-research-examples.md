# Infratex: simple research brief examples

These three examples are fictional. Their observations illustrate the format; they are not researched companies or qualified leads. Real briefs must attach source links to observations.

## Example 1 — UK lending software company

- **Business:** Sells software that helps lenders assess business loans.
- **Document task:** Its product accepts borrowers' financial statements and quarterly reports.
- **Useful detail:** Those reports contain revenue, debt and cash figures needed for credit reviews.
- **How Infratex could help:** It could pull revenue, debt and cash figures from borrower PDFs into their lending software.
- **Fit:** Worth reviewing. Confirm how the figures are entered today and who owns the document-processing work.

## Example 2 — European insurance software company

- **Business:** Builds software used by commercial insurance underwriters.
- **Document task:** Its product takes in reports of past insurance claims, known as loss runs.
- **Useful detail:** The required information includes claim dates, amounts and whether claims remain open.
- **How Infratex could help:** It could turn claims PDFs into a table of dates, amounts and claim status for their software.
- **Fit:** Worth reviewing. Check which document formats they support and whether their current extraction already meets their needs.

## Example 3 — European AI consultancy

- **Business:** Builds AI assistants for business clients.
- **Document task:** An example project lets employees ask questions about information in company PDFs.
- **Useful detail:** The assistant needs to find the right passages and tables before answering.
- **How Infratex could help:** It could make information inside the client's PDFs searchable by the AI assistant.
- **Fit:** Worth reviewing. Confirm a live client project and whether they need help processing its documents.
