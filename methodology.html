THE GROWENCY METHODOLOGY

Find the alpha.Then refine it.

We optimise for qualified conversations. Our alpha strategies connect the right market, the right reason to act and a message that earns a reply.

Talk through your funnel ↗

01 / MAP THE TRADE

Start with
the offer.

Before the list comes the question: why should this person care?

We map your offer, funnel and market. Who already buys? What problem triggers a purchase? What does your sales team need to know before a call?

We turn that into agreed qualification criteria: the accounts, roles, context and interest that make an opportunity worth your time.

What goes into the brief?

Your offer, ideal customer, buying roles, strongest proof, market context, brand voice and the definition of a qualified call or positive reply.

02 / BUILD ALPHA STRATEGIES

Find a reason
to reach out.

An angle is a specific hypothesis about what makes your offer relevant now.

We research accounts and people to find useful context: a new market, an operational bottleneck, a change in the business, or a problem your offer can solve.

Each angle links that context to a clear value proposition and a simple next step. We choose a focused audience that lets us test the idea properly.

An example of an angle

A company entering a new market may need a way to reach new buyers. The test is whether a message grounded in that expansion earns relevant interest. A signal starts the research; it does not prove the need.

Account fit Buying context Relevant message

03 / RUN THE PILOT

Small tests.
Real conversations.

Two weeks to learn which audiences and angles move your pipeline.

Our operators build the list, write the outreach, run follow-ups and handle replies. We test the strongest angles and review what prospects actually say.

We look at qualified positive replies and booked calls alongside the context behind them. A polite response and a buying conversation are different signals.

We protect your voice and sending reputation as we work. Increasing volume only makes sense when the targeting, message and infrastructure can support it.

04 / OPTIMISE FOR QUALITY

Keep what moves.
Cut what doesn’t.

The feedback loop matters more than the send count.

We review replies for fit, intent and objections, then refine the audience, angle, copy and follow-up. We stop weak angles quickly and put more attention into the ones earning qualified interest.

We report booked meetings, positive replies, the angles we kept and the ones we cut. We show what changed and why.

A booked call comes with the prospect research and conversation history, so your team can pick up where we left off.

What is measurable alpha?

The qualified interest and meetings a tested angle produces in your market. We use the pilot to establish what works for your business; we do not treat send volume or open rates as the result.

05 / AGREE THE NEXT TRADE

Proven first.
Agreed in writing.

If the pilot works, we talk about continuing.

We agree scope, qualification criteria and fees for booked calls and positive replies. Growency takes no commission on your sales.

Your team owns closing. Our operators own the work from research to conversation and handover.
