Questions before the pilot.

      
The ones we get asked on almost every first call.

    

    

      

        What does Growency actually do?
        
We run outbound for B2B companies. We research the accounts that fit, write to the person inside each one with a reason to act, send across email, LinkedIn and introductions, and answer the replies. The calls land in your calendar and your team closes them.

      

      

        How is this different from an AI SDR tool?
        
The tools gather the research. A trained operator decides who hears from you and what they read, and every message is written or edited by someone who has read the account. Replies are answered by a person who can carry the conversation forward.

      

      

        What happens in the two-week pilot?
        
We understand what you're selling and who you're selling it to very deeply. Then we create large marketing campaigns, primarily cold emails and cold DMs, send them to all of those people and get you booked calls.
We don't charge anything for the two weeks of the free trial when we get you booked calls.

      

      

        What does it cost?
        
Terms, pricing and scope are agreed in writing after the pilot, once we both know what the angles can do for your market. There is no commitment until that point.

      

      

        What do you report on?
        
Every week: meetings booked, the replies that came in, and the book of angles, with the ones we are keeping, the ones we cut and where the budget moved. No open rates dressed up as results.

      

      

        What about our domain and sending reputation?
        
Infrastructure is part of sizing the trade. We decide how much goes out, from which domain and at what pace, based on what the sending reputation can carry, so volume never comes at the cost of your inbox placement.

      

      

        What if someone asks us to stop contacting them?
        
They are never contacted again. We keep a suppression list across every campaign, and our privacy policy explains exactly what we collect and how anyone can opt out.
