# Optional writing and review

Use only when the user requests copy, after research. The supplied client brief controls facts and offer. A client writing addendum controls tone, structure, alpha strategies and examples. Neither an invented pain point nor a generic compliment is personalization.

## Semi-structured first version

Choose one approved structure per campaign. Keep the offer and CTA stable; vary only evidence-backed slots. Label a new structure as provisional until approved. Without examples, provisional defaults are:

- Plain English, short paragraphs, a direct specific observation, one commercial hypothesis/question, one relevant offer and one CTA.
- Email body 50–90 words including greeting/sign-off; subject 2–5 words. Client limits override these defaults. Do not sacrifice factual qualification merely to hit a length target.
- No invented familiarity, “I loved your work,” “impressed by,” “hope this finds you well,” “revolutionize,” “unlock,” “game-changing,” “synergy,” “10x,” or “guaranteed” unless explicitly justified/approved in context.
- No fabricated sender experience, manufactured urgency, fake audit, unapproved discount, meeting guarantee or unsupported savings.
- Avoid exposing internal scoring, insulting the prospect's following/content/team, or dumping every research detail into the opener.

Provisional slot structure: greeting → one verified observation → a relevant conditional question → approved offer/proof if useful → one low-friction CTA. Approved proof is optional, not a reason to make a long email. Do not force the company's news into a stock “congrats on funding” line.

## Keep these client distinctions

- Posting Machine: a particular founder idea, buyer topic or customer story → qualified buyer attention/conversations. A helpful content angle beats “you only have X followers.” Do not imply impressions equal sales. Email and LinkedIn can use the same hypothesis with different wording.
- Kairos: a patient offer, published coverage arrangement or reception vacancy → how enquiries are handled when the team is unavailable. Support the existing team. State no lost revenue as fact. No price until the conflicting approval notes are resolved.
- Infratex: exact document type and downstream workflow → one comparison or public-sample walkthrough. No universal accuracy/private-deployment claims. Do not promise free scope, turnaround or partner terms without confirmation.

## Claim check before style check

For each factual sentence about the prospect or client, identify supporting evidence or an approved brief claim. If it has no support, remove it or turn it into a properly conditional question. Store claim-to-evidence mapping; a list of URLs alone is not a factual check.

QC should return `pass` or `fail` plus concrete issues and edits:

1. Correct person, company, current role, segment and geography.
2. Actual observation supported by the source and date; no provider AI claim promoted to fact.
3. Relevance to the client and buyer; one clear hypothesis rather than assumed pain.
4. Approved product, offer, proof, terms and CTA with original scope preserved.
5. Word count, banned phrases, grammar and natural personalization; no placeholders or unresolved variables.
6. No contradiction with the brief, counterevidence, suppression status or human comments.

Re-read after revisions. A critique pass in the same session is an additional check, not an independent verifier. If an existing included second model is available and authorized, it can review the same evidence; do not add a paid API reviewer by default. AI-detector scores are not proof of factual or stylistic quality.

## Human review

Present final copy beside the source bullets and unresolved issues. During the pilot every outgoing row needs a real human decision. `pending`, `approved`, and `rejected` are distinct states. Do not infer approval from silence or from the user requesting research/drafting. Record who approved, when and which exact content/research version they reviewed. New factual discoveries or edits require renewed approval.

Approval means the rep accepted the draft; it does not prove contact validity or make an unsupported claim true. Keep factual QC and human approval as separate gates.
