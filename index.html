Questions before we start.

The ones we get asked on almost every first call.

What does Growency actually do?

We create and run ads on Facebook and Instagram. We agree the cost target and spending limit for each test with you, then use the results to decide what to scale, pause, or try next.

What do you test?

We start with the biggest open question. That might be which hook gets people to pay attention, which offer converts, or which creative format brings in customers at the right cost.

How do we get started?

Tell us what you sell, who buys it, and what you want the campaign to achieve. If you already run ads, we’ll use what you’ve learned to plan the first tests.

What does it cost?

Engagements start at £1,000 per month. We’ll agree the campaign scope and ad budget with you. Explore pricing.

What do you report on?

For each test, you see what we tried, what it cost, whether it passed, and what we’re doing with the budget next.

When do you scale an ad?

When it hits the cost target with enough conversions to pass the test. We check that it stays within target as spend goes up.
