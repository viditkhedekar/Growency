Growency vs Artisan

Artisan pairs Ava, its AI BDR, with a built-in contact database and deliverability tooling in one workspace. Growency is a service: operators research, write and answer, and you get the booked calls.

Choose Artisan if

  • You want data, sending and an AI rep in one workspace
  • You would rather buy software than a service
  • Cost per contact matters more than depth per account
  • You are happy for AI to write most messages

Choose Growency if

  • Your buyers are senior and a generic note costs you the account
  • You want a person to read every account first
  • You want replies answered by someone who can carry a call forward
  • You want to see what works before agreeing terms

Where Artisan is strong

Consolidation. Artisan folds the data, warmup and deliverability work that usually needs several tools into one place, with Ava doing the sending.

For a team replacing a stack of subscriptions, that simplicity is real.

Where Growency is different

Artisan automates the rep. We keep the rep human and automate the gathering around them.

That makes us slower per account and more deliberate about each one, which is the point for teams whose deals depend on a few right conversations.

Can you use both?

If Artisan already covers your high-volume segments, we can take a narrower list of accounts where the message needs a person behind it.

Two weeks, then you decide.
