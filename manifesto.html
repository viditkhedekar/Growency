THE COMPANY MANIFESTO

The hedge fund
for sales.

We invest research, judgement and human-led outbound into your pipeline. You keep the upside.

These are the principles behind the work.

See the methodology ↗

01 / THE STANDARD

Quality earns
the next call.

A pipeline is only valuable when the people in it have a reason to be there.

We build for the right conversation: a prospect who fits your market, understands why you reached out, and wants to speak to your team.

More sends are not the objective. More qualified opportunities are. We care about the interest behind a reply and the fit behind a booked call.

What we mean by quality

Account fit, a relevant person, a clear reason to act, and genuine interest in a conversation. We agree what qualifies with you before judging a campaign.

02 / THE PEOPLE

Tools do the work.
People make the call.

Good outbound needs judgement, ownership and someone who can carry the conversation forward.

Research tools help us find context. Operators decide what matters, write or edit the message, and handle the reply.

Your brand shows up in someone else’s inbox. It should sound like a person who has done the homework. We take that seriously.

We stay with the conversation through follow-ups, interested replies and the handover to your team.

03 / THE DISCIPLINE

Prove the angle.
Earn the next trade.

We map your funnel and offer, test the strongest angles, and show you what moved. If it works, we keep going.

Every campaign starts with a hypothesis about who needs your offer and why they might act now. We test that hypothesis with real conversations.

We keep the angles that earn qualified interest. We cut the ones that do not. We show you what changed, rather than dressing up activity as progress.

That is our alpha: finding the market, message and timing that move your pipeline.

04 / THE ALIGNMENT

Your trade.
Your returns.

Our job is to get the right people to your calendar. Your team closes the deal.

We charge for booked calls and positive replies. We do not take a commission or a share of your sales revenue.

The scope, qualification criteria and pricing are agreed in writing after the pilot. You can decide based on what the work has shown.
