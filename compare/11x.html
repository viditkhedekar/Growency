Growency vs 11x

11x sells Alice, an AI “digital worker” that researches, writes and follows up on its own. Growency uses tools for the research and keeps a trained person on every decision about who hears from you and what they read.

Choose 11x if

  • You want software, not a service
  • Volume across many channels is the priority
  • You are comfortable with AI writing without a person reading each account
  • Someone on your side can oversee the agent

Choose Growency if

  • You care what your brand sounds like in a stranger’s inbox
  • You want a person deciding who gets contacted
  • You would rather test for two weeks than sign for a year
  • Replies need judgement, not just a fast answer

Where 11x is strong

Reach and speed. An agent does not get tired, works every channel 11x supports, and can touch far more accounts than a human team.

For high-volume motions where each contact is cheap, that throughput is hard to match.

Where Growency is different

We think the expensive mistake in outbound is the wrong message to the right person. So the tools do the gathering, and an operator makes the call.

The pilot runs for two weeks with no commitment, which is a smaller bet than an annual software contract.

Can you use both?

Some teams point an AI SDR at the long tail and keep their best-fit accounts for a human-led approach. That split can work well.
