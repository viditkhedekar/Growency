# Quick research examples

All companies and observations below are fictional demonstrations, not verified leads. Default: about five short bullets per company, with source links/locators inside factual bullets in a real run.

## Posting Machine — fictional founder

- **Product/buyer:** Finance software for mid-market CFOs, according to the supplied demo product note.
- **Founder:** Demo data lists 2,300 personal followers; live count and posting frequency are unverified.
- **Recent LinkedIn post:** A supplied demo post from the past 30 days explains a CFO customer's reporting problem; a real run would include its published date and direct post link.
- **Potential angle:** Build on that specific post with founder-led explanations for finance buyers; this is a hypothesis to test, not proof of weak pipeline.
- **Fit:** review_needed. Buyer fit looks plausible; confirm traction and the content gap before qualifying. Do not infer weak pipeline from follower count.

## Kairos — fictional Texas dental practice

- **Practice:** Demo website describes an independent general dentist accepting new patients; ownership still needs confirmation.
- **Demand signal:** Demo page promotes implant consultations and lists a 5pm closing time.
- **Coverage:** Demo booking page offers an appointment-request form; that does not establish real-time booking or phone coverage.
- **Potential angle:** Ask how enquiries become appointments outside staffed hours, without claiming the practice loses calls or revenue.
- **Fit:** review_needed. Texas must be the chosen campaign state; confirm ownership and current coverage. Flag these gaps and move on.

## Infratex — fictional European CRE software company

- **Product/buyer:** Demo product notes describe underwriting software for commercial real estate teams.
- **Workflow:** Demo help material names rent rolls and operating statements as inputs to a standardized underwriting dashboard.
- **Signal:** A demo engineering vacancy mentions ingestion pipelines; that does not prove extraction failures or an active vendor search.
- **How Infratex could help:** It could pull tenant names and rents from their property documents into a table their underwriting software can use.
- **Fit:** review_needed. Confirm the operating country and technical owner; keep comparison scope and timing conditional on Infratex approval.
