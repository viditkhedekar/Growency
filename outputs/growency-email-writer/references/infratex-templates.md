# Infratex current email templates

Use these two templates in place of the earlier four. Replace bracketed fields with supplied research only. Choose the exact service sentence from the three ICP paragraphs below; keep it as paragraph 2 after the opening. Include both help options in each email, as short verb phrases after “We could”, retaining conditions where the workflow is unconfirmed.

Use lowercase subjects in either form: `helping you [outcome]` or `let us automate your [workflow]`. Default examples use Hey and Best regards; rotate greetings and sign-offs as specified in SKILL.md.
