# Posting Machine: three-company research

Researched 21 October 2026. Bounded research on the three supplied names.

Decisions: Simulithic needs review; Moonshot and Tsenta are outside the current B2B campaign. Founder profile counts are indexed snapshots, not live verified counts.

Decision: **review_needed**. Strong B2B workflow match; customer maturity, economics and buyer-conversion gap remain unresolved.

- **S3** Pricing page says design partners are onboarding; Starter, Team and Enterprise prices require a founder call. [Source](https://www.simulithic.com/pricing). Verification: direct; confidence: high. Observed 2026-10-21.
- **S4** Satyam publicly says the first waitlist companies are being onboarded; no paid-customer count stated. [Source](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/satyamsingh2003_we-have-worked-at-companies-like-bree-offstreet-activity-7511661332706828288-zH5S). Verification: direct; confidence: high. Observed 2026-10-21.
- **S5** Indexed Ryan profile displays about 9K followers and a claim of over 1M impressions in under three months. [Source](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ryan-ning). Verification: unverified; confidence: medium. Observed 2026-10-21.
- **S6** Indexed Satyam profile displays about 13K followers. [Source](https://www.linkedin.com/in/satyamsingh2003). Verification: unverified; confidence: medium. Observed 2026-10-21.

### Interpretation

- Buyer-role inference: engineering leaders, technical founders and QA leaders; actual audience participation not verified.
- Ryan formerly engineered at Uber/Shopify; Satyam at Ramp/Bree, per YC.
- Founder-led demo funnel and design-partner onboarding are visible; no public ARR/ACV or named paid-customer proof established.
- Ryan audience is near the upper end of the brief; Satyam appears outside the standard range.

**Angle to test:** Test whether real cross-device failure demonstrations and a founder view on verification can turn existing attention into engineering-buyer conversations. Do not pitch basic follower growth.

### Counterevidence and gaps

- They already produce founder content; low following is not established.
- YC lists two people, below the preferred 10-100 band, which is a preference rather than a hard exclusion.
- Current exact followers and complete 30/90-day activity windows
- Paying customers, retention, ARR and ACV
- Audience buyer composition and existing content-attributed pipeline
- Full funding history and current verified headcount
