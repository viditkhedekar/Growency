**74 supplied rows · 56 unique contacts/companies · 18 exact duplicate records.** The first CSV contains 50 rows and the second 24. This is six fewer input rows than the expected 80; the repeated records add no new contacts. All 74 rows are accounted for below and in the CSV.

**Research result:** 9 qualified for an exploratory document conversation, 40 review needed, 7 excluded from this campaign. 24 reports contain a proposed document-based help sentence; this includes conditional and out-of-geography examples. It is not a count of send-ready contacts. Remaining records: 0.

The current client PDF says to cast a wide net among fintech firms, with Europe as the location filter. Accordingly, firms without a confirmed document use remain visible for review. Direct lenders, investment groups, solar businesses and possible partners are tagged separately. Turkey is left for geography review; no EU-only assumption was applied.

**Reading the reports:** business and document observations are linked to the reviewed public page. Contact names, emails, job titles, employee counts and provider email labels come from the supplied Apollo exports unless explicitly stated otherwise. Their original dates were not established. A LinkedIn link is a supplied identity reference, not proof that the profile was inspected or that the role is current. Employee figures are provider estimates, not audited counts.

**Status meaning:** qualified means enough European company and document evidence for an exploratory conversation, subject to checking the contact's current role. It does not mean a qualified meeting, buyer intent, budget, available project, email deliverability or integration readiness. Review needed means a material fact or use case remains unresolved. Excluded means outside the campaign on the available company-location evidence, not a claim that no European subsidiary can exist.

Every help sentence is a proposed application. No existing errors, manual work, savings, accuracy, integrations or need to replace a provider is assumed. For companies already processing documents, discuss a difficult format or new requirement rather than presume their current system is inadequate.
