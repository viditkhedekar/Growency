Operating noteAlpha strategies

An Alpha strategy is none of the following.

  • a prompt
  • a template
  • a mass-send
  • a list you bought

It is a researched, repeatable way to identify a buyer, read their context, choose the channel, write something credible, follow up at the right moment, and turn interest into a sales conversation. Each one takes months to build.

We run a portfolio of them rather than betting an account on a single idea. The ones that produce meetings get more capital. The ones that do not get cut in week two.

The trade lifecycle

Six steps, in order, every time.

  1. ALPHA-01
  2. ALPHA-02
  3. ALPHA-03
  4. ALPHA-04
  5. ALPHA-05
  6. ALPHA-06
  1. ALPHA-01

    Source the position

    Account and signal research. We find the companies that fit and the people inside them with a reason to act this quarter.

  2. ALPHA-02

    Build the thesis

    Message architecture, written and edited by someone who has actually read the account. The structure repeats across a campaign. The specifics never do.

  3. ALPHA-03

    Size the trade

    Channel, sequence and infrastructure. How much goes where, from which domain, at what pace, and what the sending reputation can carry.

  4. ALPHA-04

    Execute

    Launch, coordinated across email, LinkedIn and introductions, so an account has more than one credible way to hear from us.

  5. ALPHA-05

    Mark to market

    Live reply monitoring. Every response is read and answered by an operator who can carry the conversation forward.

  6. ALPHA-06

    Close the position

    Weekly reporting and the iteration loop. Keep the angles that worked, cut the ones that did not, and put the budget behind the winners.

Every campaign is run by a trained operator.
