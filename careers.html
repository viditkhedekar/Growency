Learn the craft.Run the campaign.

Founding GTM Operator — Pilot Cohort

A pilot program for people who want to become go-to-market operators. Learn how modern B2B outbound works, then run a real campaign for a real client.

2–5 days learning 2–3 weeks unpaid probation 1 month paid phase

01 / WHAT THIS IS

Skills you keep.
Work you can prove.

Learn the method before you run a real pilot.

You will learn how modern B2B outbound actually works, then either prove you can do the work or learn that it is not for you. Either way, you walk away with skills you can use for the rest of your career.

This is a practical path into GTM and sales operations. Progress depends on the results you deliver, how you respond to feedback and how well you run the work.

STEP 0 / LEARNING · 2–5 DAYS

First principles.
Before first sends.

We teach you what you need to know before you run a campaign.

We provide training materials and teach modern B2B outbound from first principles. You are expected to read Founding Sales by Pete Kazanjy before you start sending.

What you will learn

  • Cold outbound fundamentals from Founding Sales.
  • Clay for list-building and enrichment.
  • Apollo for contact data and sequencing.
  • Origami for personalisation at scale.
  • Writing cold emails that earn replies.
  • Managing a campaign from first send to booked meeting.

You will access free credits on Clay, Apollo and Origami through your own accounts.

STEP 1 / PROBATION · 2–3 WEEKS · UNPAID

Win a pilot.
Run it end to end.

Find one client willing to let you run a real outbound campaign, and do the work.

You will run a pilot study for a small group of prospective clients. Build a target list in Clay or Apollo, write personalised cold emails, send them, handle replies and report on what worked.

During probation

  • Send your first 100 emails within 5 days of signing.
  • All sales calls with prospective clients go through Vidit, so we maintain brand quality while you learn.
  • You run the campaign. Vidit reviews your emails before they go out for the first 7 days.

What passing looks like

A signed pilot client and a real campaign with measurable results: replies and at least a few booked meetings.

STEP 2 / PAID PHASE · 1 MONTH

Your clients.
Your pipeline.

Once you have proven you can run a pilot, you move to paid campaigns.

You will run your own clients and pipeline, with your own commission structure. We talk through the details after probation.

The work stays practical: campaign ownership, clear reporting, feedback and qualified results.

STEP 3 / HIRE

Build a career.
Earn the next role.

If the paid phase goes well, you are officially hired.

There is room to grow into senior roles, pod lead positions and eventually the founding team.

We do not promise a specific path, but the path exists for people who do the work.

03 / WHO THIS IS FOR

Self-directed.
Open to feedback.

People who want to build a real career in GTM and sales operations.

You will fit if you

  • Write clearly and learn tools quickly.
  • Stay focused without someone watching over your shoulder.
  • Want to build a career in GTM and sales operations.
  • Are comfortable with 2–3 weeks of unpaid probation while you prove yourself.

This is not a fit if you

  • Need a guaranteed paycheck from day one.
  • Cannot handle feedback on your email copy.
  • Need a structured 9-to-5.

A short note.
A real conversation.
