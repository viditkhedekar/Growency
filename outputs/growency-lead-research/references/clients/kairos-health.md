## Commercial question

Does this established independent dental practice receive patient enquiries that need answering and booking when its team is busy or unavailable? Research the workflow and capacity to serve patients. Do not assert lost calls, lost revenue or staff overload without evidence.

## Qualification and unresolved settings

- **US only, one US state per campaign.** Ask which state before qualifying or exporting a batch. General source analysis can continue without one; do not silently choose Texas from the customer examples.
- Independent general, pediatric and selected orthodontic dental practices. The brief mentions typically 1–4 dentists; verify owner-led identity and small-group ownership. Do not equate clinic size with a measured front-desk workload.
- **Location-count conflict:** opening ICP permits small private groups of 2–11 locations; a later passage says 1–5 locations. Record this as unresolved. One to five locations is the shared provisional research band; 6–11 needs explicit review, not silent acceptance or exclusion. Do not claim the user approved the provisional band.
- Established, accepting new patients, with credible incoming-demand signals and capacity to serve. Unknown call volume/budget does not prevent a useful introduction.
- Large DSOs, non-dental organizations and practices without relevant call/scheduling need are lower priority; do not turn “lower priority” into a hard exclusion without a campaign rule.
- Identify dentist-owner/practice owner or practice/office manager. Small-group operations owners are relevant. An associate is a referral path when they cannot own the decision.

## Research specifics

Check clinic pages, hours/contact/booking, new-patient offers, services, team/ownership, location pages, recruitment and dated announcements. Maps may assist discovery; the clinic's own site should confirm the targeting observation.

- Exact office hours/time zone, booking options and published after-hours process. Closing hours alone do not prove calls go unanswered; an existing online booking service may weaken or change the angle.
- Verified new-patient promotion, implant/restorative/cosmetic service, or visible advertising. A landing page alone is not proof an ad is currently running; require an observable active ad for that claim.
- Current receptionist/scheduling/patient-coordinator vacancy, posted date and live status.
- New dentist, hours expansion, location opening or publicly stated patient growth.
- Explicit recall backlog or stated administrative burden, where actually described.
- English/Spanish services advertised by the practice. Do not infer patient ethnicity or language from staff surnames, photographs or neighborhood demographics.
- Practice-management software only if evidenced. Kairos's integration list does not establish the clinic's software, installed integration or compatibility with every configuration.
- Ownership/group membership, number of locations and relevant decision-maker identity.

Strong angle: a specific new-patient offer plus a question about booking after closing, or a live reception vacancy plus a question about coverage during hiring. Lower priority: generic “AI saves time,” speculative lost revenue, unrelated clinical trivia.

**Hypothesis:** coverage might help enquiries become suitable appointments and reduce routine work. Existing demand and clinical capacity matter: an enquiry is not an attended visit or treatment sale. Do not use public patient reviews as proof of quantified call volume or access private patient data.
