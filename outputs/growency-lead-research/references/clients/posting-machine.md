## Commercial question

Does this founder have a real B2B business, reachable professional buyers and useful expertise that could become a stronger LinkedIn revenue channel? Find evidence of product traction plus an unsolved content/distribution opportunity. A small audience alone is not a problem statement.

## Recent LinkedIn posts: priority in the quick pass

For Posting Machine, prioritize extracting the correct founder's recent LinkedIn posts, rather than spending the quick pass filling general company fields. Check the founder's personal activity before the company feed. Prefer an existing supplied post export, an available authorized LinkedIn capability or accessible public posts; use normal browser access when available. This is a best-effort collection step, not a guarantee of LinkedIn access or a bundled standalone scraper. Do not start a paid scraping or enrichment service under the zero-spend default.

- **Window:** start with the last 30 days and inspect up to the latest 3–5 visible original posts. A single accessible activity feed or supplied export is enough to read several posts; do not crawl the full history or every cofounder.
- **Collect:** short factual summary, post URL, published date where available and visible reactions/comments if useful. Distinguish the founder's original posts from reposts and company posts. Keep observation date separate from publication date. A relative date or search-index snapshot with uncertain freshness must be labelled; do not call an undated post recent as a verified fact.
- **Look for:** buyer problems, customer stories, product launches, founder opinions, sales/GTM lessons, growth or hiring, and signs of existing content expertise. Pick the post with the strongest connection to Posting Machine's offer, not automatically the newest or most liked.
- **Fallback:** if the profile/feed is blocked, make at most one focused public search for posts by the matched founder and open a promising post if accessible. If nothing recent can be verified, flag that and move on. Use older posts only when readily available and useful, label their age, and never infer inactivity from inaccessible or incomplete results. Company posts may supply a secondary signal, clearly labelled as company activity.
- **Output:** use one of the five bullets for the best recent post's topic, date and link, or the inability to verify recent posts. Connect the potential angle to that actual post in another bullet, keeping the need/pain conditional. Do not append a separate post report or turn this into a complete posting-frequency audit.

Keep this within the quick pass: the company page plus a bounded LinkedIn check normally replaces other research lanes. Follow extra post URLs only to verify the material used; stop once there is one useful supported post or the bounded fallback is exhausted. A clear campaign disqualifier can still end deeper research early.

## Qualification

- US-based B2B company; founder preferably US-based. Verify company location separately from founder location. Founder/co-founder or CEO who is demonstrably a founder.
- Existing customers and credible traction/PMF evidence; a clear ICP and functioning GTM motion. Describe the evidence rather than declaring PMF proven from logos alone. If maturity is unclear, review it.
- Buyers active and identifiable on LinkedIn: this is a hard dimension. Name buyer roles and business reasons they use the channel; distinguish category plausibility from observed buyer presence.
- Founder credibility could influence selling, trust or positioning; room for an additional content-led channel.
- Standard founder follower range 500–10,000, ideal 1,000–5,000. At 500–1,000, require excellent company fit and some activity. At 5,000–10,000, investigate a specific content-to-pipeline gap. Above 10,000 generally exclude unless a clear gap justifies an exception. Below 500 is outside the standard target: review, do not silently expand the range.
- Seed to Series A is preferred, not a universal hard exclusion. $1M–$15M funding, 10–100 employees, roughly $500K–$10M ARR and $5K+ ACV ($10K+ ideal) are strong-fit signals. Unknown estimated ARR/ACV is a gap, not permission to invent a figure.
- Look for economics that make $499/month plausible and could support $1K–$3K/month over time. Funding does not prove ability/willingness to pay. Record the basis of any economic judgment.
- Exclude supported pre-product/no-customer cases, consumer/local SMB/very low-ticket mismatches, creators/influencers and founders with an already mature, effective LinkedIn acquisition engine. Inaccessibility or missing data calls for review, not automatic exclusion.

## Research specifics

In quick mode, prioritize basic company/buyer fit and recent founder posts as above. The following is an optional menu for deeper research, not a required checklist:

- Exact/approximate followers, observation date, and whether count is followers or connections.
- Posting frequency over a defined visible window; the brief's ideal is roughly 1–4 posts/month or irregular posting.
- Recent topics, one useful post, visible engagement and whether identifiable ICP buyers participate.
- Company announcements vs buyer-relevant expertise; evidence of an established content team or mature engine.
- Recent raise, launch, GTM vacancy, new customer or new vertical and the exact source/date.

Useful combinations: a customer story plus sparse founder commentary; a recent launch plus a founder explanation of the buyer problem; GTM hiring plus evidence that the founder is still central to sales. State the commercial connection as a hypothesis. Do not tell the founder they have failed at LinkedIn, are wasting followers or lack pipeline.

**Worth mentioning:** a specific product/customer/buyer insight or actual founder post that naturally connects to the offer. **Usually internal only:** follower count, funding bracket, assumed affordability. Low following is a selection signal and can make an abrasive opener; a useful content idea is often a better conversation starter.

## Sources and tiers

YC is the primary sourcing route, with VC portfolios and other B2B ecosystems also allowed. Affiliation is not a qualification requirement. The original brief's approximately 2,000-account source mix is a campaign plan; it does not authorize discovery or list-padding on every invocation.

- Tier A: strong supported fit across maturity, economics, LinkedIn buyers, founder audience, content gap and founder-led GTM.
- Tier B: good potential with one or two uncertain dimensions; keep `review_needed` when those uncertainties affect acceptance. Tier B does not automatically pass export gates.
- Tier C: borderline; do not fill volume targets with weak leads.

The fields in section 13 of the original are an inventory for requested detailed research or export. Do not fill every field in quick mode; choose the strongest specifics for the five-bullet summary. Preserve known fields and mark important unknowns. Include full evidence IDs, dates and distinct cold-email / LinkedIn hooks only when needed for the requested mode.
